# Usability Test Plan

Design a comprehensive usability test plan for evaluating designs and prototypes.

## Core Components

- **Research Foundation**: Define clear objectives and 3-5 specific research questions.
- **Methodology Selection**: Choose moderated or unmoderated, remote or in-person, think-aloud protocol.
- **Participant Strategy**: Define screening criteria, target 5-8 participants per segment, establish recruitment approach.
- **Task Design**: Develop 5-8 realistic scenarios with clear success criteria and estimated completion times.
- **Measurement Framework**: Track task success rates, completion times, error frequencies, SUS or SEQ scores, and satisfaction ratings.
- **Facilitation Approach**: Script introductions, task delivery, strategic probing questions, and structured debriefing.
- **Data Management**: Establish what to record, create observation templates, develop note-taking frameworks.
- **Synthesis Process**: Plan how findings will be synthesized and issues prioritized for design iteration.
- **Pre-Testing Verification**: Pilot test checklist to validate before formal sessions.

# Gesture Patterns

Establish standards for touch and pointer interactions. Eight primary gestures: tap (selection), double-tap (zoom), long-press (context menus), swipe (navigation), pinch (scaling), rotate (directional adjustment), drag (repositioning), pull (refresh). Discoverability: visible affordances, initial-use hints, always provide non-gesture alternative (button/menu). Feedback: immediate visual response, progress indicators, threshold visualization, completion confirmation. Technical thresholds: 10-15px minimum distance, velocity requirements for flicks, directional locking, cancel zones. Conflict resolution: direction-locking after initial movement, system gestures take priority. Accessibility: every gesture must have a non-gesture alternative, support switch and voice control, respect reduced-motion, enable single-handed operation, provide undo for destructive actions.

# Diary Study Plan

Plan longitudinal research through diary studies to reveal how people actually behave in their everyday environments.

## Key Components

- **Research Foundation**: Clarify the behavioral patterns or experiences to investigate over extended periods.
- **Study Design**: Recruit 8-15 participants with defined screening criteria and compensation. Establish a 1-4 week timeline. Develop daily and event-based entry prompts combining open-ended and structured questions.
- **Data Collection**: Capture information through multiple formats — photos, video, text, and audio recordings.
- **Engagement Strategy**: Schedule mid-study check-ins through brief interviews or pulse surveys. Conduct thorough onboarding covering participant briefing, practice entries, and tool orientation to minimize dropout.
- **Analytical Approach**: Develop a coding and thematic synthesis process to identify patterns across all diary entries and across participants.

## Process

1. Define research questions that require longitudinal observation (not answerable in a single session)
2. Design entry prompts: daily structured prompts + event-triggered open prompts
3. Recruit and onboard participants; run a practice entry before the study begins
4. Monitor engagement during the study; follow up with participants who fall silent
5. Conduct mid-study check-in interviews to clarify entries and surface emerging themes
6. Analyze entries systematically: code by theme, cross-participant pattern, and timeline
7. Conduct exit interviews to contextualize diary entries with participant reflection

# Diary Study Plan

Plan longitudinal research through diary studies to reveal how people actually behave in their everyday environments.

## Key Components

- **Research Foundation**: Clarify behavioral patterns or experiences to investigate over extended periods.
- **Study Design**: Recruit 8-15 participants, establish 1-4 week timeline, develop daily and event-based entry prompts combining open-ended and structured questions.
- **Data Collection**: Capture information through multiple formats — photos, video, text, and audio.
- **Engagement Strategy**: Mid-study check-ins and careful onboarding to maintain participation and reduce dropout.
- **Analytical Approach**: Develop a coding and thematic synthesis process to identify patterns across all diary entries.
